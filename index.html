Approach

I sit between what clients need and what actually ships.

Forward-deployed. I work directly with the client, turn their requests into specs, build them, and come back for review with the people who actually use the thing. No PM layer in between. The feedback loop is hours, not sprints.

The most dangerous thing about AI coding is not that it fails, it is that it succeeds confidently in the wrong direction. So I build the guardrails, not just the features.

Citation accuracy fell from 96% to 82% after a release. Fixed-token chunking was splitting clauses mid-sentence.

Structure-aware chunking plus a confidence threshold put it back at 96%.
